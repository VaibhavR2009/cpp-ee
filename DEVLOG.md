## 📖 How This Log Works

1. **Newest entries go at the top** — right under *The Log* header, so the latest day is always easy to find.
2. **One entry takes ~5 minutes.** If it takes longer, I'm overthinking it.
3. **Honest > polished.** Bugs, confusion, and zero-progress days all belong here. That's the point.
4. **Every week ends in a Review.** Every month ends in a Recap. Both take 10 minutes, max.
5. **The Bug Graveyard is sacred.** Every bug that cost me over an hour gets a tombstone and a lesson.
